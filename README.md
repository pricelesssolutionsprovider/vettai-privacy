Privacy Policy for VettAI
Effective Date: November 12, 2026
Developer: VettAI

1. Overview
VettAI is a 100% offline local document indexing and search application for Android. We respect your privacy and built VettAI so that your documents, search queries, and personal data never leave your device.

2. Data Collection & Internet Access
VettAI does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any personal data, usage analytics, crash logs, or file contents. The application operates entirely offline without external network communication.

3. Device Storage & Permissions (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)
To provide its core functionality of indexing and searching documents and images across your local storage (.pdf, .docx, .doc, .txt, .xlsx, .xls, .xlsm, .csv, .ppt, .pptx, .pps, .ppsx, .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .heic), VettAI requests All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE).

All text extraction, Optical Character Recognition (OCR), and search indexing occur strictly on-device.

Extracted text is stored solely in a local database inside VettAI's private app storage on your phone.

Deleting the index inside VettAI ("Clear all") or uninstalling the app permanently removes the local database.

4. Third-Party Services
VettAI contains no advertisements, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no external cloud integrations. On-device text recognition runs locally on your hardware.
